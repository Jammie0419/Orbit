- **Single-source conclusions** — one page (or one snippet) does not make a
  fact; say "according to one source" instead of asserting.
- **Copy-paste blocks from a page** into the answer without synthesis.
- **Asserting numbers/dates from snippets** — snippet text truncates; open
  the page for the actual value.
- **Dropping provenance** — claims without sources cannot be verified by the
  owner; record the URL next to the claim.
- **Ignoring contradictory evidence** — when sources disagree, report the
  disagreement and why you picked a side.
