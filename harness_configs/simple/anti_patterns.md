- **Ceremonial tool chains** — calling tools to look busy on a question you
  can answer from context.
- **Half-answering a real task** — a request that needs files/web/memory is
  not a chat; escalate instead of giving a partial answer.
- **Over-explaining** — long disclaimers, restating the question, or
  summarizing the obvious.
- **Dropping continuity** — ignoring `chat_history` / `recent_tasks` when the
  owner references earlier turns.
